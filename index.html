Our Nuzlocke Rules

  1. Catch only the first eligible wild encounter per area.
  2. Dupes clause: you may reroll a species or evolutionary line already obtained this run.
  3. Nickname every Pokémon.
  4. A fainted Pokémon is permanently retired from the run.
  5. Do not enter a Gym battle above its level cap.
  6. No healing or battle-enhancing bag items in trainer battles; held items are allowed.
  7. If all usable Pokémon are lost, mark the run as wiped.
  8. Starter is a free encounter; no encounter cheats during the run.
Gym levels vary by Quetzal version and settings. Enter your verified next Gym and level cap manually.
